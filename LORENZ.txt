examples/lorenz -- a scientific test case that chunks hard, across ranks.

WHAT IT IS

Lorenz's 1963 convection model, marched 2000 steps with classical RK4.  The
Jacobian of the final state with respect to the initial state is the
TANGENT-LINEAR PROPAGATOR M(T) -- the object at the centre of 4D-Var data
assimilation and of Lyapunov analysis, and a genuinely hard thing to obtain by
finite differences, because the system is chaotic: a perturbation grows like
e^(0.9t), so the step small enough to stay linear is too small to stay
accurate.  That is the practical argument for AD on this problem.

WHY IT CHUNKS

A long time integration of a 3-variable system makes a tape that is DEEP and
NARROW -- exactly the shape the checkpointing machinery is for.  At the
default 180 kB budget the 2000 steps break into 148 partitions, dealt round
the ranks:

    np=1   148
    np=2    74  74
    np=3    49  50  49
    np=4    37  37  37  37
    np=7    21  22  21  22  21  22  21

AND IT ASSERTS THAT, rather than observing it.  Fewer than eight partitions
is a failure, and so is every partition landing on one rank when np>1.  An
example that silently stops chunking stops testing anything; in a pipeline
there is a second way to stop testing -- chunk, but onto a single rank -- and
both are failures here.  Verified by running with a 100 MB budget, where both
assertions fire and the example exits non-zero.

TWO CHECKS, NEITHER A FINITE DIFFERENCE

 1  THE VARIATIONAL EQUATION.  dM/dt = J(u)M integrated alongside the state in
    plain doubles, no AD anywhere.  For a Runge-Kutta method the
    tangent-linear of the scheme IS the scheme applied to the variational
    equation with the same stage values, so this agrees to MACHINE PRECISION
    rather than to discretisation order.  Observed 4.5e-15 to 8.4e-15 at every
    rank count, against a 1e-11 tolerance.

 2  LIOUVILLE'S THEOREM.  Lorenz's field has constant divergence
    -(s+1+b) = -13.667, so det M(T) = exp(-13.667 T) exactly.  An analytic
    identity binding all nine entries at once -- nothing agreeing with another
    numerical propagator would satisfy it by accident.

THE SECOND CHECK FINDS ITS OWN LIMIT, AND SAYS SO

det M is the product of the three singular values: one grows like e^(0.9T)
while the product shrinks like e^(-13.67T), so the determinant is a tiny
number formed by cancellation between entries that are not tiny.  Computing it
costs eps*||M||^3/|det M| in relative terms:

    T=0.25   5e-14        T=1.0   4e-7
    T=0.5    1e-11        T=2.0   larger than 1

Measured at T=2: the Liouville residual was 8e-2 at one rank and 2.0 at four,
while the tangent-linear check held at 3e-15 at every rank count.  The
determinant disagreeing across rank counts there is NOT the library computing
a different answer -- it is the same one-ulp reassociation the invariants
suite pins, amplified by 1e14.

So the example works that bound out from the Jacobian it actually produced,
adds the RK4 discretisation error, and asserts Liouville only where double
precision can resolve it.  Past that point it prints the check as NOT
RESOLVABLE with the numbers and moves on.  A test that quietly widens its
tolerance until it passes is worse than no test.

    ./lorenz [steps] [budget_bytes] [dt]        default: 2000 180000 0.00025

Try `mpirun -np 2 build/bin/lorenz 2000 180000 0.001` to see the unresolvable
branch, and `... 2000 100000000 0.00025` to see the chunking assertions fire.

HOW TO INSTALL IT

With git, from inside Boltzmann:

    git am 0003-examples-lorenz.patch

Without git: create examples/lorenz/, put main.cpp in it, put
examples/lorenz/Makefile.new there as Makefile, and replace examples/Makefile
and the top-level Makefile with the two Makefile.new files here (lorenz is
added to the fast group, to `make ranks` and to `make sanitize`).  README and
the design note are updated too.

boltzmann.bundle here is refreshed to seven commits, the last being this one.
